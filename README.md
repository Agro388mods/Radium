Radium is a concept and early-stage Minecraft mod focused on late-game End technology built around unstable, radioactive materials and simulation-based mechanics.
Originally suggested on Minecraft Feedback by a friend, this repository serves as a design hub while development skills are still in progress.

The goal of Radium is to introduce powerful but controlled End-game systems that feel experimental and rewarding, without turning Minecraft into a full automation or tech mod.

This repository contains:

Core design documents and feature ideas

Planned mechanics and system concepts

Progress tracking and development notes

Balancing, performance, and gameplay considerations

Community feedback and iteration history

Design principles

End-game progression first

No instant or infinite storage early on

No world-wide instant transfer systems

Performance-aware mechanics only

Utility and gameplay depth over raw power
