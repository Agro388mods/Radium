<h1 align="center">Radium</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Experimental End-Game Minecraft Mod Concept</strong><br>
  Late-game End technology • Radioactive materials • Simulation-based mechanics
</p>

---

<p>
  <strong>Radium</strong> is a concept and early-stage Minecraft mod focused on
  <em>late-game End technology</em>, built around unstable, radioactive materials
  and simulation-based mechanics.
</p>

<p>
  Originally suggested on <strong>Minecraft Feedback</strong> by a friend, this
  repository serves as a <strong>design and planning hub</strong> while development
  skills are still in progress.
</p>

<p>
  The goal of Radium is to introduce <strong>powerful but controlled End-game
  systems</strong> that feel experimental and rewarding — without turning
  Minecraft into a full automation or tech mod.
</p>

---

## 📦 What This Repository Contains

<ul>
  <li>Core design documents and feature ideas</li>
  <li>Planned mechanics and system concepts</li>
  <li>Progress tracking and development notes</li>
  <li>Balancing, performance, and gameplay considerations</li>
  <li>Community feedback and iteration history</li>
</ul>

---

## ⚙️ Design Principles

<ul>
  <li>End-game progression first</li>
  <li>No instant or infinite storage early on</li>
  <li>No world-wide instant transfer systems</li>
  <li>Performance-aware mechanics only</li>
  <li>Utility and gameplay depth over raw power</li>
</ul>
